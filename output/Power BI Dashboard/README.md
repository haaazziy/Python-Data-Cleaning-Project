Superstore Sales Dashboard – Power BI

Project Overview

An interactive three-page Power BI dashboard developed to analyse sales performance, profitability, product performance and customer insights.

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Average Discount
* Sales by Region
* Sales by Category
* Monthly Sales Trend

2. Sales Analysis

* Sales by Region
* Sales by Market
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Category Performance

3. Product & Customer Insights

* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Customers by Sales
* Profit by Sub-Category
* Sales by Segment

Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI

* Data Visualization
